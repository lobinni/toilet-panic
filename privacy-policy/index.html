Summary

TOILET PANIC does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device, works offline, and has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Game settings — audio volumes, graphics quality and camera preferences. Stored locally on your device so the game remembers your choices. This never leaves the device.
  • Optional diagnostic export — if you deliberately choose to export a technical report, it is written as a local file. It is not uploaded anywhere. You decide whether to share it.

The app does not read your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone or camera, and does not request those permissions.

Information collected automatically

None. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service and no telemetry. No advertising identifier is accessed. No device identifier is collected or transmitted.

Network use

Gameplay is fully offline and does not require an internet connection. The app makes no requests to our servers or to third-party servers, because there are none.

Third-party services

TOILET PANIC uses no third-party service that receives user or device data. The app is built with standard open-source software libraries that run locally on your device and do not transmit information.

If you download the app from Google Play, Google's own Play Store processes your download and purchase/installation activity under Google's privacy policy. That processing is performed by Google, not by this app.

Children

The game contains cartoon object destruction, no gore and no online interaction, and it collects no data from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about suitability, please contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data held on any server to retain or delete. Local settings stay on your device until you remove them. You can delete everything at any time:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → TOILET PANIC → Storage on your Android device.
  2. Tap Clear data, or simply uninstall the app.

This permanently removes all locally stored game settings and any diagnostic file created by the app.

Security

There is no account, no server and no transmitted data, so there is no remote database that could be breached. Locally stored settings are protected by your device's standard application sandboxing.

Your rights

Because we never receive personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf. All data created by the app is already under your direct control on your own device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will also be disclosed in the app's Google Play listing.
