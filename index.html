About the game

TOILET PANIC is a first-person 3D cartoon survival challenge. One bathroom, one hammer, and one toilet that refuses to stay broken. Everything you smash can push the room closer to disaster — and the toilet fights back.

TOILET PANIC is released for Android. There is no browser version to play here — this page is an introduction to the game.

What you get

First-Person Chaos

You are in the bathroom with nothing but a hammer.

Destruction Challenge

Chip, crack and shatter your way toward destroying the toilet.

Unpredictable Accidents

Every smash can set off a bathroom disaster.

Water & Electrical Hazards

Break a pipe and flood the floor. Break the light and spark the wire.

Dangerous Chain Reactions

Disasters connect. The bathroom escalates.

Toilet Regeneration

It heals. It resists. It does not want to die.

Sarcastic Death Voices

Lose, and get mocked by a voice that really enjoys it.

Offline & On-Device

No account. No internet. No tracking. No mercy.
